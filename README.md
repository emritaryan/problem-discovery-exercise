# Problem Discovery Exercise

## Company selected

**Myntra** - an Indian online fashion and lifestyle marketplace.

## Business understanding

### What does the company do, and who pays for it?

Myntra sells fashion, beauty, and home products online. Customers pay for purchases, while sellers pay platform and advertising fees.

### How does it make money?

Myntra earns through seller commissions, platform fees, advertising fees, delivery charges, and sales of its own brands.

### What must it get right to run well?

Product availability, accurate listings, fast delivery, easy returns, competitive pricing, and customer retention.

### Where do problems occur?

Key issues include product-quality dissatisfaction, high return volumes, fraudulent returns, and inventory shortages for high-demand products.

### What data does it collect?

Searches, clicks, purchases, product views, location, preferred sizes and brands, return reasons, ratings, and delivery data.

## AI opportunity recommendations

| Feature | Problem it solves | Type of AI | Data needed | What it improves | Metric it will impact |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Image Verification | AI-generated images and incorrect product listings can cause product mismatch and returns. | Computer Vision | Listing images, product attributes, and customer photos | Listing accuracy and customer trust | Reduce product-mismatch return rate by 10% |
| Virtual Try-On Studio | Customers are uncertain how clothing, accessories, or colours will look on them. | Generative AI + Computer Vision | User-uploaded image, product images, and product attributes | Purchase confidence and shopping experience | Increase cart-to-conversion rate by 15% |
| Conversational Search Assistant | Customers spend too much time scrolling and may not find the right product. | Generative AI | Product catalogue and attributes, ratings, inventory, delivery availability, and customer reviews | Product discovery and search relevance | Increase search-to-purchase conversion by 15% |
| Personalised Product Recommendations | Customers may miss relevant products in a large catalogue. | Recommendation AI | Browsing history, wishlists, purchases, ratings, preferred brands, and price range | Product discovery and repeat purchases | Increase average order value |

## Recommended first feature

**Image Verification** should be prioritised first because it can reduce product-mismatch returns, improve customer trust, and lower reverse-logistics costs.
